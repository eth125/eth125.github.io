Projects

Five systems, one team

All built at OPCC IT Department since December 2023. These are internal company apps, so the pictures are my own sketches of each idea, not real screenshots.

OPSMobile_Store

Retail point of sale and store suite

  • Canteen checkout with exact money maths: totals round half-up to the nearest 0.10, which ended checkout discrepancies.
  • One printing engine for Bluetooth Classic, BLE, network and USB receipt printers, with automatic reconnection and reprints.
  • Offline-first batch sync that survives dropped connections without half-written stock data.
  • Barcode-driven stock receiving and stock counts for purchase orders.
KotlinESC/POSBluetooth SPP/BLESQLiteZXing

OPSMobileNetPay

Cash payment distribution with face verification

  • Started the codebase from the first commit: MVVM architecture, SQLite schema and security layers.
  • Real-time face verification that pairs Google ML Kit face detection with a custom TensorFlow Lite embeddings classifier.
  • Tuned for a Samsung Galaxy A05s: frame processing held near 15 FPS plus an inference cache, which significantly reduced false rejections.
  • Liveness detection against spoofing, and database constraints that stop a payment being handed out twice.
KotlinML KitTensorFlow LiteCamera2MVVM

OPSMobile_Main

Computer vision scanner for logistics

  • A custom YOLOv8 licence plate detector running on the phone through TensorFlow Lite, replacing a slower full-screen processor.
  • Hand-written post-processing for the model output: non-maximum suppression, bounding box maths and cropping.
  • Zero-tap scanning. The camera captures by itself once a plate is found.
  • Text reassembly that reads multi-line, scrambled or reversed plates and filters out noise.
KotlinYOLOv8TensorFlow LiteOCRNMS

OPSMobile_Payroll

Field operations and workforce suite

  • NFC badge verification, so staff check in with one tap.
  • Pay slips printed on the spot from a mobile thermal printer.
  • Photos stored the right way up on every phone brand, fixed with post-capture rotation.
  • An admin force-sync that pushes new biometric data from cloud to device in one atomic SQLite transaction.
Kotlin / JavaNFCESC/POSSQLiteGitHub Actions
